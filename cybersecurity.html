Termux / Linux Sandbox

No PC? No problem. Practise real terminal commands right in your browser — nothing to install.

u0_a123@localhost: ~ — Termux Sandbox
$
Try: help, pwd, ls, cd, mkdir test, touch file.txt, cat notes.txt, pkg update, ping google.com, clear
